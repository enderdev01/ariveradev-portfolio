## Platform

web

## Users
Two audiences, confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-25:
- **Nearshore software clients** (US and LatAm teams) evaluating OniLabs for custom web, mobile and API work. The home page and /servicios speak to them.
- **Small and mid-size businesses in Lima**, dental clinics first, that already have clients (Google reviews, WhatsApp sales) but whose website is broken on mobile, slow, or missing. They usually arrive on a phone after a one-to-one WhatsApp or email from OniLabs about their own site, and check who wrote and whether the offer is real. /rediseno-web speaks to them.

## Product Purpose
OniLabs is a web development studio in Lima (founded January 2026). For SMBs it updates or builds the website so it works on a phone, puts WhatsApp at the top, and leaves domain and accesses in the client's name. Success for the SMB page: the visitor writes on WhatsApp.

## Positioning
"Your website is yours": redesign the site the business already has, keep its domain and working content, and put domain, accounts and accesses in the client's name. Results are measured in the client's own tools (WhatsApp messages starting with "Vengo de la web", Google Business Profile clicks), never promised.
